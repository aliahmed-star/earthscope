About the data and risk classifications

NASA POWER provides daily gridded environmental data for the selected coordinates; these are not in-situ weather-station observations. The dashboard requests T2M (°C), RH2M (%), WS10M (m/s converted to km/h), and PRECTOTCORR (mm/day), with daily dates requested in UTC. Parameter products and source datasets can vary. See the NASA POWER Daily API documentation, parameter dictionary, and NASA POWER referencing guide. Risk levels use prototype rules and are not official warnings or validated disaster predictions. Incident actions only change a local demonstration status; they do not contact emergency services.
